# Nothing wallpaper

Procedural Glyph Matrix field: pure black (`#000000`) with a regular lattice of
faint grey dots (`#262626` / `#161616`), step 28 px, at 3456 × 2234. Generated
locally with a deterministic Python script (`tools/make-nothing-wall.py`) — no
AI imagery, no third-party photograph. Matches the Nothing OS / Glyph rice
desktop language (black, white dots, one red elsewhere in the UI).

Fonts shipped under `fonts/glyph/` are from the [glyph-rice](https://github.com/Nebelung-cloud/glyph-rice)
distribution (OFL): Doto, Space Grotesk, Space Mono.
