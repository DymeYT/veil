## Why this one
- No characters, creatures or statues. It shows only the palace roofs, the lake, cascading waterfalls, sakura and a far-off temple on a cloud-wrapped hill.
- It is the most "landscape" Sekiro frame available: a deep, layered panorama with a calm, flat blue-teal sky across the top third.

## Visual weight and top edge
- Weight sits in the lower half: the waterfalls (bottom 40%, bright white-blue) and the roofs in the middle band. The top ~30% is near-uniform sky.
- **Top 70px:** average `#2d577e` (luma ~78). It is even from left to right (`#33618a` → `#264d72`), a calm mid blue with no detail, so it works fine under a translucent bar.
