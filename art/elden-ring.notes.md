## Why this one
- Environment only: no characters, creatures or figures. The scene is a fortress, rock, autumn conifers, hanging roots, god-rays and a misty ravine.
- It reads as a vista. The keep sits on the left third and the right side falls away into a fog-filled valley with ruined colonnades, so there is real depth behind tiled windows.
- The Scadutree's canopy, with ember-gold glints, fills the upper right. That ties in with the "guidance" gold accent.
