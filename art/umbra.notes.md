# Umbra wallpaper

NASA astronaut photograph ISS073-E-611649, taken from the International Space
Station on September 7, 2025, during the partial phase of a total lunar eclipse.
Credit goes to the Expedition 73 crew and the NASA Johnson Space Center Crew
Earth Observations Facility and Earth Science and Remote Sensing Unit.

Veil crops the 4928 × 2768 published image to 3600 × 2328 at x=570, y=170,
then resamples it to 3456 × 2234. The bright lunar edge stays clear of
desktop controls. The original is a Nikon D4 photograph. Veil adds no
imagery to the photo.

- [NASA Earth Observatory image and credit](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/by-the-warm-light-of-the-moon-154814/)
