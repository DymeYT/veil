# Foundry (Factorio) wallpaper

- **Source:** Wube Software official rollup factory artwork, https://cdn.factorio.com/assets/img/artwork/rollup-base.jpg (4429x3115), kept at `candidates/factorio/rollup-base.jpg`. Rights: Wube Software; personal desktop use.
- **Crop:** 4169x2695 from x=0, y=420 (drops the Factorio wordmark at top right), downscaled with Lanczos to 3456x2234. No upscaling, no repainting.
- **Why:** a sprawling megabase seen from above. Detail is spread evenly — belts, rails, smelter rows, assembler blocks, lakes — so no single subject competes with windows, and any gap between windows still reads as "the factory". Unmistakably Factorio.
- **Bar edge:** busy texture at the bottom where the bar sits; the bar is an opaque strip, and the top edge sits under the notch band.
- **Previous:** Vulcanus lava frame from the Space Age trailer (`candidates/factorio/vulcanus-2026-10-28.*`), replaced because it read as an anonymous lava texture.
