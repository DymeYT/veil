## Why this one
- No characters, creatures or statues are visible. It is pure architecture and landscape: Irithyll's gothic cathedral and rooftops, snow-covered forest, mountains, and Anor Londo on its cliff, under a churning night sky.
- It is a sweeping, layered vista with the darkest top edge of all four themes. This is DS3's best-known environment shot.
