# Night City wallpaper

- Artwork: Night City skyline at night (Data Inc. and Softsys towers, the Arasaka tower at right) from Cyberpunk 2077 imagery.
- Source: frame at 0:50 of the live-wallpaper clip the user had chosen in Backdrop (copied to art/live/night-city-skyline.mov before Backdrop was removed). Real game imagery, not AI-generated (Cyberpunk 2077, © CD Projekt Red), but the clip's origin (official footage vs. a capture by the Backdrop catalogue's uploader) is unverified. No rights claimed; personal desktop use.
- Crop/output: 3342x2160 window from the 5070x2160 frame (x=600, keeps the Softsys tower), scaled to 3456x2234, JPEG q2.
- Visual weight: central tower cluster and the magenta billboard; the sky band at top is dark and calm for the bar.
- Palette: near-black navy sky, cyan/electric-blue facade light, magenta and violet billboards, a little warm yellow (Arasaka sign).
- The previous AI-generated image is kept in art/ai-generated/night-city.jpg for reference only.
